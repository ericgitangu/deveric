# Overview

A centralized operations interface that brings together activity monitoring and administrative workflows. It is designed to help staff review live work, handle exceptions, and keep service operations organized.

## Capabilities

- Present operational activity in a consolidated dashboard.
- Support account and workflow administration.
- Surface exceptions that need staff attention.
