# Overview

A web-based assistant that streams responses to users, preserves conversation history, and provides a status view for operational metrics. A small native extension handles selected text-processing tasks, with a fallback implementation for environments where the extension is unavailable.

## Capabilities

- Stream responses and render formatted content.
- Persist conversations for later access.
- Support multilingual interactions.
- Show service health and latency metrics.

## Stack

Python, Rust, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Redis, and containerized services.
