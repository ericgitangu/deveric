# Overview

An assurance platform for teams that build or operate machine-learning systems in healthcare. It combines model robustness checks, risk tracking, and tamper-evident audit records to make security and compliance evidence easier to review.

## Capabilities

- Evaluate models against adversarial and robustness scenarios.
- Organize evidence, findings, and remediation across isolated workspaces.
- Preserve audit events in a tamper-evident record for later review.

## Stack

Rust services, Python model-evaluation workers, a TypeScript web application, and PostgreSQL.
