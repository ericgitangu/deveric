# Context and problem

Healthcare teams need a disciplined way to assess model behavior and organize the resulting findings without treating an evaluation score as sufficient assurance. The platform brings robustness checks, risk ownership, and an audit trail together so reviewers can understand what was tested and what remains unresolved.

## Engineering approach

- **Evaluation as a workflow.** Adversarial and robustness scenarios produce evidence that can be connected to findings and follow-up remediation.
- **Separated workspaces.** Findings and evidence are organized by workspace, supporting distinct teams and review contexts.
- **Reviewable history.** A tamper-evident audit record preserves important evaluation and governance events for later examination.
- **Polyglot execution boundary.** A typed web application, Rust services, and Python evaluation workers separate user interaction from model-testing workloads.

## Outcomes and trade-offs

The project makes model assurance a traceable engineering process rather than a one-time test report. Combining specialized services adds operational complexity, so the public-facing design emphasizes explicit evidence and risk tracking rather than unsupported claims of regulatory certification.

## Stack

- Rust services and Python model-evaluation workers
- TypeScript web application
- PostgreSQL-backed evidence and audit records
- Adversarial testing and model-governance workflows
