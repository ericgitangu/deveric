# Overview

A web application for tracking goods as they move through a supply chain. Inventory records, user permissions, and status updates are connected to verifiable blockchain events.

## Capabilities

- Authenticate users through a connected wallet.
- Manage role-based access to inventory and tracking workflows.
- Follow product movement with real-time status updates.
- Verify selected records against blockchain transactions.

## Stack

Next.js, React, MongoDB, and Web3 integrations.
