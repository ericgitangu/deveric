# Overview

A set of automated checks and reporting workflows for reviewing repository health. The goal is to make quality findings actionable and preserve a clear record of follow-up work.

## Capabilities

- Inspect repositories for maintainability and delivery concerns.
- Summarize findings in a reviewable format.
- Track remediation over time.
