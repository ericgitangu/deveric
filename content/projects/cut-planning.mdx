# Overview

A planning workflow for turning customer measurements into practical board layouts and consistent quotes. It tracks reusable offcuts, applies configurable material and service prices, and produces a printable plan for fulfilment.

## Capabilities

- Optimize part placement while accounting for cut width and grain direction.
- Calculate quotes from a workshop's price list.
- Convert uploaded photos or documents into draft cut lists for human confirmation.
- Track jobs from intake through planning, quoting, and invoicing.

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, document generation, and image-assisted data entry.
